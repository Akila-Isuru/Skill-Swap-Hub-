The Deep Dive

Everything you need to know about Swapping.

01. Setting Up Your Digital Identity

Your journey begins with building a comprehensive profile that highlights your expertise and your curiosity. Unlike traditional social media, SkillSwap Hub focuses on what you can contribute to another's growth.

  • Define Your Mastery: List skills where you have at least intermediate knowledge.
  • Identify Your Gaps: Be honest about what you want to learn. Whether it's a coding language or a soft skill.
  • Verification: Our community thrives on trust. Users with detailed bios and linked portfolios often get 3x more requests.

02. The Intelligence Behind the Match

We don't believe in endless scrolling. Our smart algorithm analyzes the "Supply" and "Demand" of skills within the community to suggest the most efficient partnerships.

If you teach Python and want to learn Photography, the system specifically looks for Photographers who are struggling with Python. This mutual need creates a strong foundation for a successful swap.

03. Structuring the Exchange

Once a match is made, the "Swap Request" serves as your formal introduction. We recommend setting clear expectations before you begin.

  • The Discovery Call: Use our chat feature to decide on the curriculum.
  • Time Banking: Decide if you will swap hour-for-hour or task-for-task.
  • Session Execution: Use your preferred tools (Zoom, Google Meet, or Physical meetups) to conduct the teaching sessions.
